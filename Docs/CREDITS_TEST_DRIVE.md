# Test drive: third-party assets

## Traffic vehicles (`Assets/Resources/TestDrive/Traffic/*.glb`)

- "Generic passenger car pack" by comrade1280, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/20f9af9b8a404d5cb022ac6fe87f21f5, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- "Generic civil service vehicles pack" by comrade1280, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/8ff2a13f30914932a70c7950cfa58465, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Taken from the squared-up, atlas-packed build in the open-source project skywright (https://github.com/anielsud/skywright, `assets/traffic/`). Changed for this project: split into one file per vehicle (most detailed level only), each with its own cropped texture, a body colour baked in, wheels and lamp markers named for the wheel rig and the night lights, turned nose-first.

## Trees (`Assets/Resources/TestDrive/Trees/*.glb`)

Generated with ez-tree by Daniel Greenheck (https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree), MIT licence.

## People

The seated drivers, the extra walkers, the talking groups and the bench sitters are copies of the scene's own pedestrians (Remy, Mixamo).

## Drivable concept car

The showroom's hero car, "Car Concept" (Unity Technologies, public domain CC0; glTF conversion in the Khronos glTF Sample Assets), is also offered for a test drive as the "Aurelle GT Concept".

## Shop interiors

The ten shop atlases in `Assets/Resources/TestDrive/Shops/` were drawn procedurally for this project.
